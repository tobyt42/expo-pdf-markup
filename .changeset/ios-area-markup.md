On iOS, drag a rectangle to highlight or underline any page, including scans. This replaces text-selection highlighting and underlining; marks no longer snap to text lines.

Switch `annotationMode` to `"none"` to scroll, zoom or turn pages by touch. Programmatic page changes and book-mode keyboard controls still work while marking.

Rebuild your native iOS app to use this behavior.
